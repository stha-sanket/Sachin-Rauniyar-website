Musical Journey

Sachin Rauniyar is a celebrated Nepali singer with over 35 years of musical excellence, beginning his journey in 1987 with his first school stage performance. Known for his versatile style across pop, modern, classical, and rock music, he has released several successful albums including Nasha and Sparsh.

While performing across countries such as Nepal, India, Singapore, Germany, the USA, and Japan. Honored with prestigious awards like the Hits FM Award and Bindawasini Award, his passion for music, songwriting, and live performance continues to inspire audiences around the world.

Three decades of soulful music, timeless melodies, and a legacy that continues to inspire.
